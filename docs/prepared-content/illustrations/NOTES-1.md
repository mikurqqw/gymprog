# Batch 1 asset notes

Prepared 2026-11-17. Content is machine-assisted and has not been reviewed by a coach, clinician or native Kazakh speaker. No review approval is claimed.

## Integration

- `../launch-content-1.json` contains textual replacements only for `ex-0` through `ex-30`. Each exercise has RU/KK/EN summary, setup, 3–4 execution steps, breathing, three mistake/fix pairs, safety, filming advice and cues.
- Competition squat and bench cues are retained where specific. Conventional-deadlift cues omit the unrelated Romanian-deadlift sentence. Test descriptions do not change test prescriptions or progression.
- No variations are proposed; no injury permissions are inferred. Bodyweight squat and bridge explicitly avoid hand support. Holding loads, machine handles, bicycle handlebars and forearm support remain arm loading.
- `manifest-1.json` lists ten original diagrams: `ex-0`, `ex-1`, `ex-2`, `ex-3`, `ex-4`, `ex-5`, `ex-7`, `ex-8`, `ex-9`, `ex-12`. The seated pulldown illustrates the cable option of `ex-7`; the seated curl illustrates the seated option of `ex-8`.
- SVGs are 640×360, transparent, and use `currentColor` for anatomy plus lime `#c5f36b` with contrasting outlines. Inline the trusted local SVG or otherwise provide theme colors: an SVG loaded through an `<img>` cannot inherit the host page's `currentColor`.
- Give the integrated image its localized exercise name and a localized description of the two positions. The visible labels intentionally contain only START and END; the asset itself does not provide a translated accessible name.
- These are schematic movement cues, not measured joint angles, competition judging aids, individual technique prescriptions or biomechanical validation. Especially the oblique Pallof-press view cannot establish correct three-dimensional alignment. Coach review remains pending.
- Chromium rendered the ten diagrams in dark and light colors. The first visual pass prompted contrasting outlines around lime arrows and equipment. Updated previews are `preview-1.png` and `preview-1-light.png`.
